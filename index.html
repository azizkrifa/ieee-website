About us

Who we are

IEEE FSM Student Branch connects students at the Faculty of Sciences of Monastir who are passionate about electrical engineering, computer science, and technology at large.

We organize technical workshops, speaker sessions, hackathons, and competitions throughout the year, and give members the chance to take on real responsibility — from leading a project to running an event from scratch. Membership comes with access to the global IEEE community, its publications, and its network of student branches worldwide.
